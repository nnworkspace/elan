# Onboarding , User Journey and Functional Requirements

## Source Reference
**Draft Digital Euro Scheme Rulebook V0.9** , Section 3.5.2.1 (Onboarding), Section 3.3 (Identification), Section 3.4 (Authentication), and Annexes B1 (Illustrative user journeys) / B2 (End-to-end flows).

## Purpose
This document captures **what onboarding must achieve** from the user's perspective: gaining access to digital euro services through a PSP. It is the upstream input that the rulebook codifies into binding onboarding rules (`20-rulebook/onboarding-rules.md`, rules `ONB-*` / `AM-011-*`).

> User journeys are **illustrative** of intended outcomes; they are not themselves binding (cf. rulebook FUR.01). The binding force lives one layer down, in the rulebook.

---

## 1. User journey , onboarding an individual user

Onboarding can take place **online** in the PSP's remote environment, through the **digital euro app**, or **in person** during the PSP's service hours (the last to support financial inclusion).

High-level flow (User → PSP → DESP), summarised from Rulebook Figure 3-2:

1. The user asks its PSP to provide access to digital euro services.
2. The PSP onboards (if the user is unknown) and authenticates the user, performing identification and KYC/AML checks per applicable regulation.
3. The PSP requests the **DESP** to generate a **Digital Euro Account Number (DEAN)** mapped to the PSP.
4. The DESP generates the DEAN and records its mapping to the PSP.
5. The DESP returns the DEAN and confirms the mapping to the PSP.
6. The PSP links the DEAN to the user, activates the user's payment instrument(s) or acceptance solution(s), and sets up liquidity-management and notification preferences (if requested by the user).
7. The PSP informs the user of successful onboarding and shares the DEAN.

The detailed end-to-end flow is the concern of Annex B2 (not reproduced here).

## 2. Functional requirements

- **REQ-ONB-01 , Access through a PSP.** A user gains access to digital euro services only through a scheme participant (PSP), which is responsible for onboarding.
- **REQ-ONB-02 , Identification before activation.** The PSP performs identification and KYC/AML checks in compliance with applicable regulation before a digital euro account is activated.
- **REQ-ONB-03 , DEAN allocation.** Each onboarded user is allocated a Digital Euro Account Number (DEAN): a pseudonymised, PSP- and country-agnostic identifier, generated by the DESP and mapped to the requesting PSP.
- **REQ-ONB-04 , Optional alias.** A user may voluntarily register an **alias** (a phone number, one-to-one with the DEAN) to be addressable for online payments; the PSP verifies the alias (e.g. one-time password).
- **REQ-ONB-05 , Single identity for individuals.** The system must support enforcement of a single digital euro identity per natural person, so that holding limits apply per person rather than per account.
- **REQ-ONB-06 , Switch, not multi-home.** Where an individual already holds a digital euro account, the journey must offer **switching** to the new PSP rather than holding accounts at several PSPs simultaneously.
- **REQ-ONB-07 , Inclusion.** Onboarding and access must remain available to users without a smartphone or with low digital skills, through in-person and adapted means.

## Downstream , how the rulebook codifies this

These requirements are made binding by the onboarding rules in [`20-rulebook/onboarding-rules.md`](../20-rulebook/onboarding-rules.md):

- REQ-ONB-05 → `ONB-01` / `AM-011-001` (one user, one account).
- REQ-ONB-02 → `ONB-02` (intermediary KYC responsibility).
- REQ-ONB-03 → `ONB-03` (digital euro account creation and identifier linkage).
- REQ-ONB-06 → `ONB-04` / `AM-011-004` (switching, not multi-homing).
